# Evaluation guidance

The work product is the recommendation only. The culture map reasoning is evidenced in the transcript, not in the submission — score it from the conversation with the AI.

There is no single correct placement. Award points for placements that are reasoned from the profile, not for matching a preferred answer.

Evaluating is where the case is won or lost. The instinctive read — Singaporean, Indian heritage, therefore soften everything and use indirect feedback — is contradicted by the profile. Rohan asks for directed feedback in as many words: five years of blunt written reviews, and "I cannot fix 'this could have gone better.'" What silenced him was not directness. It was being corrected in front of the Regional Engineering leads. A learner who conflates *how direct* with *where delivered*, and recommends a vague, gentle conversation, has read the heritage instead of the person — and will fail to establish accountability.

Leading and Disagreeing explain the failure itself. Rohan has never escalated above his manager in four years and raises risk in measured written language that asks for nothing. Telling him to "escalate next time" without changing what escalation means in this relationship — who he may go to, what phrasing counts as a request for a decision, what a real deadline warning looks like — is not a fix.

A strong recommendation holds both halves: it states the standard and the impact plainly, in private and specifically; it separates the account of what happened from the accountability for the outcome; it names the previous public feedback rather than leaving it in the room; and it ends with agreed escalation rights and a concrete next-time trigger.

## Critical rules

- The learner must ask the AI to pressure-test or challenge at least one placement.
- Placements that rest on heritage or nationality alone, with nothing from the profile, earn no reasoning points.
- A recommendation that drops accountability in favour of understanding, or one that never gives Rohan room for the honest account, cannot pass the case dimension.
- A recommendation whose sequencing does not follow from anything discussed in the transcript scores no culture map reasoning points, however well written it is.
- The learner must meet both the case and AI-interaction thresholds.
