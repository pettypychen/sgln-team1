# Rohan Krishnan — Senior Programme Manager

## Basics

- Singapore-born, age 34. First generation in his family born and raised in Singapore; both parents migrated from India before he was born.
- Senior Programme Manager for two years; four years with the company. Reports directly to the Head of Product Operations.
- Runs programmes that depend on Regional Engineering, Customer Support and two upstream data teams.

## Background

- Engineering degree in Singapore, then five years at a large American infrastructure company where reviews were blunt, quarterly and written down.
- Has told his manager he prefers that: "Tell me exactly what was wrong. I can fix a specific thing. I cannot fix 'this could have gone better.'"
- Was promoted early on the strength of two difficult launches he delivered on time.

## How Rohan works

- Documents everything. Keeps a decision log and a dependency tracker that nobody asked him to keep.
- Raises risks in writing, inside status updates, in measured language — "there is some pressure on the Monday date" — rather than by calling a meeting or naming a person.
- Has never once escalated above his manager, in four years, including on the two launches that nearly slipped.
- Answers direct questions fully and honestly. Volunteers much less, especially about other teams and about decisions his manager made.
- Went quiet for several weeks after a design review last year where his manager corrected him in front of the Regional Engineering leads. He completed the work; he stopped offering opinions in that forum.
- Is respected by the receiving teams and often does their chasing for them.

## Recent context

- In the final week before the handoff, requirements changed three times and two upstream teams delivered late.
- Rohan's status updates that week flagged "a risk to the Monday handoff" twice. Neither update asked for a decision, a revised date, or help.
- He has not raised the feedback he found abrupt. His manager heard about it from a colleague.
