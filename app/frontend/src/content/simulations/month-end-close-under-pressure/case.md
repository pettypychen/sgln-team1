# Month-End Close Under Pressure

You are an early-career accounting associate at fictional Straits Grove Services. It is 4:00 p.m. on 31 July and the finance manager needs a close-readiness update by 5:00 p.m.

Use only the supplied ledger, checklist, and manager notes. You do not need Singapore tax expertise. Do not claim to post or approve entries. Label proposed corrections and investigations clearly.

## Required work product

1. An exception table with source reference, issue, amount, impact, and next action.
2. Recalculated affected totals, with assumptions labelled.
3. A prioritized close-blocker list.
4. A finance-manager update under 250 words.

Use AI to inspect all three artifacts. Check its calculations and classifications rather than accepting them at face value.
