# The Next Seven Weeks

Everything on Serene's calendar between now and the talent review that puts her in front of someone two or more levels above her. Seven opportunities. She cannot prepare properly for more than two or three, and one of them cannot be prepared for at all.

Her working week is already full. Any preparation comes out of evenings, or out of the consolidation.

---

## Week 1 — Monthly global engineering call

Four hundred attendees. Dana hosts. Forty-five minutes of updates, fifteen minutes of open Q&A.

11:00pm Singapore time. Serene has dialled into eighteen of these and spoken in two.

The Q&A is where Dana forms views — she has said so on the call. Roughly six questions get asked each month, usually by the same five or six people, four of whom are in Seattle. Ryan asks one most months.

*Cost: an hour on the night, plus whatever preparation the question takes. Recurs in weeks 4 and 7.*

---

## Week 2 — Dana's Singapore visit

Dana is in the Singapore office for two days.

**The floor walk.** Ninety minutes, unscheduled, Dana moving through the engineering floor with the site lead. Serene will be introduced. Realistically this is four to ten minutes of unstructured conversation, and it cannot be scheduled, scripted or guaranteed to happen at all.

**The APAC leadership dinner.** Fourteen people, three hours, Dana at the table. Seating is not assigned. Serene has been invited. At the last one of these she sat with her own team and left before dessert.

*Cost: the dinner is an evening. The floor walk costs nothing and can be prepared for in ten minutes — or missed entirely.*

---

## Week 3 — Skip-level with Marcus

Twenty-five minutes, video, scheduled six months ago. Her first with him since the consolidation was announced.

She sets the agenda. The last two were headcount and attrition — both useful, both forgettable.

This is the only time in the seven weeks she has Marcus's undivided attention, and Marcus writes the slate.

*Cost: the meeting exists either way. What varies is what she brings to it.*

---

## Week 4 — The Corvus deprecation

Not a meeting. A decision.

Corvus is a legacy service that three regions depend on and nobody owns. Its removal is on the consolidation plan with no name against it. Marcus has asked for a volunteer twice on the global call and has had no takers.

Whoever takes it is visible to all eleven people in the review for reasons that are entirely about the work. They also inherit a decade of other people's decisions, in the middle of a consolidation, with a team that is already asking whether it still has jobs.

Volunteering is a two-minute act. Doing it is nine months of real load.

*Cost: two minutes now. Substantial and lasting thereafter.*

---

## Week 5 — Architecture review

Serene presents the APAC platform migration outcome to the platform leadership group. Twenty-minute slot, twelve people, Marcus and Priya both in the room, Dana not.

She has run this migration for two years and finished it four months early and under budget. It is the strongest piece of work she has.

She is fourth of five presenters. Ryan presents third.

*Cost: preparation is substantial if she reworks it; the slot exists regardless.*

---

## Week 6 — Quarterly business review

Wei Han presents the APAC line, as always. Serene attends and does not speak.

Wei Han has said that if she wants five minutes of his slot to present the migration outcome herself, he will give it to her. He has not offered twice.

*Cost: asking costs nothing. Taking the slot costs preparation, and costs Wei Han some of his airtime.*

---

## Week 7 — Monthly global engineering call

The last one before the review. Same shape as week 1.

The consolidation will be the main agenda item. Attendance will be higher than usual and every one of the eleven will be on the call.

*Cost: as week 1, in the week when everyone is watching.*

---

## Constraints worth knowing

- Serene's own leadership team meets weekly and is currently absorbing the consolidation announcement. Two of her four directs are actively interviewing elsewhere.
- She is not senior enough to create a new forum. Everything above already exists on someone else's calendar.
- Nothing here is secret or unusual. Ryan Boyd has access to the same seven weeks, minus the timezone.
