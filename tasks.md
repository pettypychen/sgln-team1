## Overall Risk Summary

| Phase | Load Level | Biggest Risk | Mitigation |
|-------|-----------|-------------|------------|
| 1 – Launch | 🟡 Medium | No consensus on problem statement in 2 days | Send topic shortlist 48 hrs in advance |
| 2 – Research | 🟡 Medium | Stakeholder access / interview scheduling | Book sessions in Week 1; desk research as fallback |
| 3 – Ideate | 🟢 Low | Ideation sessions stall without pre-work | "Come with 3 ideas" prompt 3 days before session |
| 4 – Prototype | 🔴 High | Build + write compete for same people; scope creep | Lock prototype scope in Week 1; use Vibe-code tools |
| 5 – Test | 🟡 Medium | No test participants ready in time | Recruit during Phase 4; use async testing tools |
| 6 – Demo Day | 🔴 High | Lead bottleneck on whitepaper + pitch | Distribute writing ownership by Aug 31 |

**Phase 4 and Phase 6 are the two most overloaded phases.** Both require the full team to produce multiple high-quality deliverables in parallel under part-time constraints. Plan for these phases with extra check-ins and consider dropping lower-priority polish if the timeline compresses.
